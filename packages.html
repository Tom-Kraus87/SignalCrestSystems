Terms

Scope, revisions, payments, and exclusions

What does a fixed price cover?

The deliverables listed for that package, with nothing hidden. The price changes only if you approve added work in writing.

How do starting prices and custom quotes work?

A starting price is the minimum for the smallest version of a service. Advanced websites, e-commerce, and custom quotes are scoped individually, and you get a written scope and price before work begins.

What counts as a revision round?

One consolidated set of feedback on a design or build stage, which we then apply. The Landing Page, Starter Business, and Professional packages include 2 rounds. The Advanced Website includes 3. Extra rounds are billed at $100/hour.

What is post-launch defect correction?

We fix anything that doesn't work as agreed in your scope: 14 days for the Landing Page and Starter Business packages, 30 days for the Professional and Advanced packages. It doesn't cover new features, content changes, or additional revisions.

How does payment work?

Standard website packages: a 50% deposit to start and the remaining 50% before launch. Larger projects use milestone payments agreed in writing. Monthly plans are billed in advance. Hourly work has a 1-hour minimum, then 15-minute increments.

What isn't included?

Unless listed in your package or quote: copywriting, photography, logo or brand design, hosting, domain and platform fees, paid plugins or apps, legal or compliance review, and after-hours work.

How are add-ons and change requests handled?

Extra pages, features, and integrations are quoted, or billed at $100/hour, before work begins. Nothing is added to your invoice without your written approval.

What do you need from me?

Your text, images, logo, and account access by the agreed dates. If content arrives late, the launch date moves by the same amount.

Not sure which option fits?

Tell us what you need. We'll recommend the right package or service, with a written scope and price before any work begins.
