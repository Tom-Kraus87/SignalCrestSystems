Engagements

How engagements work

Every engagement starts with clarity on scope and pricing. Build and verification apply when the work includes delivery of a site, application, automation, or testing package.

  1. Discovery

    We review your goals, systems, and constraints so recommendations fit how your business actually operates.

  2. Written scope and pricing

    You receive a clear written scope and price before work begins — fixed package, hourly estimate, or custom quote as appropriate.

  3. Build and verification

    When the engagement includes delivery work, we build in reviewable steps and verify results before handoff. Consulting and guidance engagements may stop at recommendations.
